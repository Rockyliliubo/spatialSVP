# Main-figure source map

The manuscript uses six main figures. Figure 1 is a schematic with the reported
Pt-1A call counts; Figures 2–6 are generated directly from result tables under
`analysis/results`.

| Figure | Subject | Primary source |
|---|---|---|
| Figure 1 | Absolute and competitive nulls; matched-reference construction | `figures/make_figure1_publication.py`; Pt-1A counts from `direct_method_comparison/GSM8452847_Pt-1A/call_counts.csv` and `GSM8452847_Pt-1A_v0.2.0/discrimination_by_arm.csv` |
| Figure 2 | Global-null calibration, planted-signal detection and Pt-1A method comparison | `method_benchmark/benchmark_summary.csv`; `direct_method_comparison/GSM8452847_Pt-1A/call_counts.csv` |
| Figure 3 | Patient-positive fractions and cross-cohort recurrence | `cohort_residualization_v1/recurrence_by_cohort.csv`; `recurrence_summary.csv` |
| Figure 4 | Composition sensitivity for total spatial organization | `composition_primary_none_v1/results_long.csv`; `adjustment_summary.csv` |
| Figure 5 | Signal removed by residualization | `residualization_diagnostic/paired_summary.csv` |
| Figure 6 | Replication across three human DLPFC donors | `cross_tissue_dlpfc_donors_v1/donor_summary.csv` |

Figure 1 is generated by `figures/make_figure1_publication.py`; Figures 2–6 are
generated by `figures/make_figures2_6_publication.py`. Both scripts resolve paths
relative to the repository and write vector PDF/SVG, 300 dpi PNG and 600 dpi
LZW-compressed TIFF files to `figures/`.

Clinical associations and the single-section tissue panel are exploratory
analyses. The main figures use the residualization diagnostic and DLPFC donor
replication.
